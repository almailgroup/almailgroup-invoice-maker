# Fonts

The `.ttf` files in this folder are subsets (Latin, Greek, Cyrillic,
punctuation and currency symbols) generated by `scripts/subset-fonts.sh`
from the Google Fonts releases published as `@expo-google-fonts/*` packages.

All of them are licensed under the [SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org):
